## Topics covered

- **Linear programming:** Modeling, transformations, geometry, and the simplex method.
- **Duality & integer optimization:** Duality theory, sensitivity analysis, LP relaxation, and branch-and-bound.
- **Optimality & convexity:** Unconstrained and constrained optimality, KKT conditions, and convex optimization.
- **Nonlinear optimization:** Algorithms for nonlinear problems.
