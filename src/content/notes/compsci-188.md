Introduction to artificial intelligence notes from UC Berkeley, covering search, planning, sequential decision-making, and probabilistic reasoning. A compiled PDF and the original LaTeX source are available below.

## Topics covered

- Uninformed and informed search, heuristics, and A* search
- Constraint satisfaction problems and backtracking
- Adversarial search, minimax, and alpha-beta pruning
- Markov decision processes and reinforcement learning
- Bayesian networks, inference, and sampling
- Decision networks, value of information, and POMDPs
- Hidden Markov models, particle filtering, and Naive Bayes
