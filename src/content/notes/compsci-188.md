## Topics covered

- **Search & planning:** Uninformed and informed search, heuristics, A*, constraint satisfaction, backtracking, minimax, and alpha-beta pruning.
- **Sequential decisions:** Markov decision processes, reinforcement learning, decision networks, value of information, and POMDPs.
- **Probabilistic inference:** Bayesian networks, inference, sampling, and Naive Bayes.
- **State estimation:** Hidden Markov models and particle filtering.
