## Topics covered

- **Discrete-time Markov chains:** Transition probabilities, hitting probabilities and expected hitting times, recurrence, transience, and communicating classes.
- **Long-run behavior:** Stationary distributions, limiting behavior, and time reversibility.
- **Continuous-time processes:** Markov chains, generator matrices, Poisson processes, and birth-death processes.
- **Queueing:** Queueing systems, Little’s law, and queueing networks.
