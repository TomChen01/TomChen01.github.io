Stochastic process notes covering discrete- and continuous-time Markov models, Poisson processes, and queueing systems. A compiled PDF is available below.

## Topics covered

- Discrete-time Markov chains and transition probabilities
- Hitting probabilities and expected hitting times
- Recurrence, transience, and communicating classes
- Stationary distributions, limiting behavior, and time reversibility
- Continuous-time Markov chains and generator matrices
- Poisson processes and birth-death processes
- Queueing systems, Little's law, and queueing networks
