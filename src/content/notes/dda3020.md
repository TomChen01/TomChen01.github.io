## Topics covered

- **Foundations:** Probability, information theory, linear algebra, and optimization.
- **Supervised learning:** Linear and logistic regression, support vector machines, tree-based methods, and neural networks including CNNs and RNNs.
- **Model evaluation:** Overfitting, underfitting, the bias–variance tradeoff, and performance evaluation.
- **Unsupervised learning:** K-means, Gaussian mixture models, and expectation-maximization.
